How the model works

What the app computes for an exercise, step by step, and where every number comes from. The general method is the same for all exercises of a kind; the second half lists this exercise's own set-up, variants, strength data and sources, read straight from its data file.

What it is and isn't. A teaching tool built on statics: forces and torques in balance at each position of a slow lift, with published body and strength data. Values marked estimate or "TODO" in the data aren't from a checked source. It shows why an exercise is hard where it is and how equipment and positioning change that; it doesn't measure a person, and it isn't medical or training advice.

Further detail

What each model approximates, what broke and was fixed, and how the results were checked against measurements: model limits (on GitHub). The code and data are open: github.com/erikeppinger/force-curve-lab.
